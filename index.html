Working methodology

A structured process from opportunity to verification.

The exact workflow depends on the client, niche, available assets, budget, and campaign objective. No fixed link count is promised before research.

STEP 01

Understand the niche

Clarify audience, target pages, competitors, goals, and assets.

STEP 02

Investigate patterns

Review visible links and infer the acquisition mechanism.

STEP 03

Qualify prospects

Score relevance, editorial fit, legitimacy, and practical value.

STEP 04

Personalize outreach

Use a specific, credible angle and respectful follow-ups.

STEP 05

Verify and report

Check live status, destination, context, and documented outcome.
